UPDATED HEALTH SYSTEM

What changed about medical gear?

CONFIRMED / HEALING

Body parts, not each wound

Treatment now applies to entire injured body parts. The health display also gives more useful information about injury severity and what can be treated.

CONFIRMED / SUPPLIES

Volume-based consumables

Blood bags and drinks consume only the amount needed instead of a fixed charge. Plan capacity with that change in mind.

CONFIRMED / BLEEDING

Revised bleeding system

Bleeding was reworked to allow more time to deal with combat before treating. Bandages can treat multiple body parts; tourniquets and hemostats stop bleeding across body parts.

CONFIRMED / CRAFTING

Stims are craftable

Do not plan a vendor stop assuming you can buy Stims. They moved to crafted production in 0.5.

CONFIRMED / AI THREATS

Cover must stay flexible

Some AI throw grenades and may finish downed players. Bring enough medical supplies, avoid static cover for too long, and consider squad support.

CONFIRMED / ACCESS

Hacking devices open routes

Electronic locks can require a compatible tier of hacking tool. Bring the right device when your task route depends on restricted access.

FOUR PRACTICAL LOADOUTS

Pack around the objective

Material collection

Priority: bag capacity and survivability. Carry a reliable low-cost rifle, simple armour and appropriate medical items. Leave spare space and watch weight when collecting Steel, Glass or Construction Material.

Tier 3 / Tier 4 assault

Priority: ammo, armour coverage and medical redundancy. Expect harder AI, grenades and electronic security. Avoid bringing an untested rare gun as your only viable weapon.

Crafting / hacking run

Priority: confirmed tool tier, task item space and an extraction plan. Electronic access can be a progression requirement; do not confuse a physical key with a tiered hacker.

Smuggling operation

Priority: destination, time and cargo. Prepare resources before accepting; you may become a target in PvEvP or face AI opposition in PvE. Do not overload your pack with optional loot.

EQUIPMENT CHECKLIST

Before heading to the LZ

01

Coverage and durability

Check your helmet and carrier for the damage level and coverage actually shown in-game, not a saved 0.4 spreadsheet.

02

Ammo and magazines

Bring the right calibre, compatible magazines and enough ammunition for the destination and likely fights.

03

Treatment

Bring wound treatment, bleeding control and other medical supplies appropriate to the new body-part system.

04

Task tools

Reserve storage for materials, mission items, hacking tools and smuggling requirements before you set out.
