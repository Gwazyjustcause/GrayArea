COPY THESE, THEN ADAPT

Four useful loadouts

01CHEAP / STARTER TOWN

Replaceable AKM kit

  • AKM with the best 7.62×39 you actually have
  • Modular Operator Carrier Gen II: IIIA, front/back/sides, 4.2 kg
  • Gunfighter 14: 9 slots, 0.9 kg
  • Basic light + healing; get out rather than “winning” every fight
Purpose: learn routes with low loss pressure
02GENERAL LOOT RUN

Economy workhorse

  • AK-74/AK-12 with 5.45 BP where possible
  • EXFIL helmet: IIIA, 1.18 kg, Handshake R2
  • AMP12: 20 slots, 2.63 kg, Handshake R3
  • Red dot or 2.5–4× sight depending on the destination
Purpose: fill a bag without risking a trophy kit
03STRONG ALL-ROUNDER

DDM4 assault kit

  • DDM4 with penetrating 5.56 ammunition
  • Pantsir 2.0: III, front/back/sides, 12 kg—use when protection beats speed
  • Rush 72: 25 slots, Handshake R4
  • Specter 1–4×, M600 light and a suppressor if your platform allows it
Purpose: contested POIs and organised squads
04ENDGAME / LONG-RANGE

Deliberate DMR kit

  • M14/M1A, AK-308 or SVD with a variable optic
  • Phantom Type 2: III++, front/back/sides, 8.704 kg
  • Delta Backpack: 36 slots, 2.63 kg, Turncoat R4
  • Bring rangefinding, healing and enough ammunition to extract—not enough to siege a city
Purpose: long lines, high-risk targeted runs

DARK ROOMS, NIGHT RUNS & POINT FIRE

Lights and lasers

Early light

NcStar LED FlashLight is sold by Gunny R1 and weighs 0.085 kg. It is the easy early answer for dark interiors.

Default upgraded light

Surefire M600 Scout unlocks at Gunny R3 and weighs 0.147 kg. Use it as your practical upgraded utility light.

Cheap laser

ADE Advanced Red Laser is Gunny R2, only 0.032 kg and listed at −1% handling. Use a laser for very close point-fire reference; it does not replace an optic.

Combined upgrade

Steiner CQBL-1 is available from Gunny R2/Banshee R3. Choose it if you want a more capable laser setup and accept more weight.

Lights and lasers are utility choices, not automatic power upgrades. In PvP they can reveal you; turn them on for dark interiors, target identification and deliberate night movement.
