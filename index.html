RECOMMENDED PATH

New player priorities

1

Use cheap local guns

Run an AKM in starter areas. Ammunition and replacement rifles are easy to recover, so deaths cost less.

2

Build every trader to level 2

Then prioritize Gunny for ammunition and weapons, followed by Handshake for armour.

3

Loot around objectives

Do not ignore missions, but treat nearby safes, jewellery boxes, computers and weapon crates as the payday.

4

Upgrade when ammo supports it

Move to AK-74/AK-12, then DDM4. Save specialist DMRs for when losing one will not empty your account.
