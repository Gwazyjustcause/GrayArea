THE UPDATED PLAN

Four priorities for returning players

1

Keep your old progress

0.5's soft wipe retains rank, tasks and vendor ranks, but resets stash and money. Choose the transferred character's mode carefully.

2

Start a Hideout project

Select a station upgrade, track its requirements, and stop selling components you need for construction.

3

Relearn the world

Major Combat Outposts changed, AI are more aggressive and Tier 4 areas carry greater risk. Reassess older routes.

4

Verify your gear sources

New firearms are partly unlock- and loot-driven. Confirm trader stock and prices in the current game instead of trusting 0.4 tables.
