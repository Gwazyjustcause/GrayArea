IN THE FIELD

Small decisions, big difference

01

Check your stash

Before selling, see whether the Hideout planner still needs an item. It aggregates unfinished upgrades and subtracts current inventory.

02

Respect weight and slots

Heavy construction materials have a real movement cost. Split long supply runs into manageable trips instead of overloading for marginal value.

03

Don't rely on old COP routes

Major outposts were decommissioned and minor ones redistributed. Re-check extraction and resupply points before following a 0.4 itinerary.

04

Extract when the goal is met

A completed shopping list has value even without a jackpot. Protect the materials rather than chasing every remaining container.
