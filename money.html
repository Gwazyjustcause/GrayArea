WHAT TO DROP FIRST

Backpack rules that actually work

KEEP

1×1 valuables

Cards, watches, jewellery, phones, medical stimulants, access cards, high-tier ammo, scopes and suppressors.

KEEP

High-ticket 1×2 items

Rare spirits, evidence, blood bags, expensive medical tools and small military devices.

CONDITIONAL

Weapons & armour

Keep if they upgrade you, contain valuable optics/suppressors, or if you have space. Strip the optic/suppressor when the base gun is not worth carrying.

DROP FIRST

Low-value bulk

Food, water, common ammunition, basic medical items and bulky junk—unless the run itself needs them for survival.

Keys are not just $59–$90 sales: a key can be worth far more as access to a high-value room. Keep new keys until you know what they open; do not sell them purely because the vendor price looks low.

MISSIONS VS LOOT

The practical answer

MISSIONS

Do them when they unlock something

  • New region or combat-outpost access
  • Vendor rank, stock or blueprint route
  • Key equipment you cannot otherwise buy
  • A task happens to pass a good loot area
LOOT RUNS

Do them when you need money

  • Cash per hour is the only priority
  • Your vendors are already progressing
  • You know a low-risk repeatable route
  • You have a backpack worth filling
