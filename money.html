FIELD ECONOMY / UPDATE 0.4

Loot beats
missions.

If the only objective is money, a focused loot run is currently more efficient. Missions still matter because they unlock regions, stock and reputation—but they are progression with a cash bonus, not the main farming method.

A practical threshold for ordinary runs. Replace lower-value items as you find jewellery, electronics, evidence or compact weapon parts.

Unlocks first

When missions win

Do tasks that open the next region, improve a vendor, or unlock ammunition and gear that make later runs safer.

Community earnings are not guaranteed. Server population, loot respawns, keys, risk and your unlocked regions change the result.

LOW-FRICTION ROUTE

India 2 money loop

BACKPACK LOGIC

What to prioritize

Always inspect

Jewellery, evidence, electronics, access cards, rare military devices, optics, suppressors and expensive compact attachments.

Take conditionally

Complete weapons, armour and backpacks when you need the upgrade, when their resale is known, or when empty space is abundant.

Replace first

Food, common ammunition, low-tier magazines and bulky junk. Keep survival supplies only in the quantity your current run needs.
