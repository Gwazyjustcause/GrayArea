THE BIG SHIFTS

Six things to know before deploying

01 / PROGRESSION

The Hideout matters

Laboratory, Electronics Workshop and Utility Room stations consume materials gathered around Lamang. Production and crafted tools add alternatives to earning money solely from loot.

02 / ACCESS

Hacking is real progression

Three tiers of hacking devices open compatible keypads and card readers. Some restricted areas require the right device; recipes must be unlocked before crafting.

03 / MONEY

Old cash routes are less reliable

High-value loot is rarer. Consider materials, crafting, production and smuggling. Old high-roll item prices are references, not guaranteed 0.5 values.

Three Tier 4 destinations

RLSL-1, Lakevale Sanctuary and Northern Reach are high-risk new locations. Major Combat Outposts are no longer capturable; smaller ones were redistributed.

WHAT TO DO FIRST

A practical return-to-Lamang plan

01

Check your character

0.5 uses a soft wipe: rank, completed tasks, apparel, vendor ranks and statistics carry over; gear, locker contents and cash do not. Your existing character's PvE/PvEvP mode choice is irreversible.

02

Pick a Hideout target

Do not hoard everything blindly. Choose a station level or a crafting tool, then collect its exact resource requirements. Mark what you own in the shared-stash planner.

03

Keep a supply run separate

Visit smaller places, stashes and resource crates when gathering materials. Avoid burning expensive ammunition in Tier 4 areas just to get a few basic components.

04

Unlock before you smuggle

Confirm production capacity and materials before taking a timed contract. PvEvP deliveries can attract other factions; in PvE the threat is AI instead.

MAP / LOOT / ROUTES

Where the new priorities lead

OFFICIAL / CURRENT

RLSL-1 · Lakevale · Northern Reach

Three new Tier 4 points of interest. Expect tougher encounters and electronic security; old Tier 3 farming advice is not a safe assumption here.

COMMUNITY-REPORTED / CHECK SPAWNS

Smaller POIs and supply crates

Players report useful material crates at huts, farms and peripheral points of interest. Availability varies with loot cycles and other players.

COMMUNITY-REPORTED / CHECK SPAWNS

YBL-1 components

One October 24 report highlights copper and electronics around communications, refrigerant in the kitchen, and dynamo in an air room. Treat this as a lead, not a guaranteed route.

OFFICIAL / 0.5

Major COPs changed

Major outposts were decommissioned as capturable COPs, minor COPs moved, and Vulture is now accessible from normal vendor access, including base camp.

NEW TOOLS & ACTIVITIES

Build, produce, smuggle

Electronics Workshop

Disassemble or assemble components and eventually craft hacking devices. The recipes are unlock-dependent. Treat specific recipes as in-game data until corroborated.

Laboratory

Production and medical crafting make chemicals and related Hideout materials useful, including items used in contraband operations.

Utility Room

Supports power production for equipment and other rooms; additional functionality may be added later.

Smuggling / Hunting

Contracted deliveries create time-limited objectives, payouts and reputation. Opposing players may pursue smugglers in PvEvP. There is also a PvE version with AI opposition.
