TRACK MATERIALS

0.5 / VENDORS AND UNLOCKS

Trade with
a plan.

Rogue Ops expanded how vendors fit into progression. Vulture has become a regular vendor, while crafting, task unlocks and new guns mean 0.4 vendor-priority charts need checking.

UNLOCKS CHANGE

The developer confirms that some new weapon presets can be unlocked by completing tasks. A community report from 23 October lists additional new guns in vendor inventories; exact levels, presets and stock should be checked in-game. Do not treat preview-era “loot-only” statements as universal rules.

TRADER ROLES

Seven vendors, updated context

WEAPONS / AMMO

Gunny

Still a natural choice when you need firearms, ammunition and weapon components. Community 0.5 report: Benelli M4 appears in Gunny's stock after relevant unlock conditions.

ARMOUR / CARRIERS

Handshake

Traditionally a primary source of protective equipment and packs. Check current ranks and inventory after 0.5 before relying on an older item-by-rank table.

MEDICAL

Lab Rat

Medical access still matters, but treatment mechanics changed. Confirmed: Stims must now be crafted and cannot simply be purchased from vendors.

EQUIPMENT / FIREARMS

Artisan

Useful for broader equipment. Community 0.5 report: UMP45, G3 and FAMAS have appeared in Artisan's stock following relevant progression.

AK PLATFORM / OTHER GEAR

Turncoat

Historically relevant for eastern-pattern weapons and equipment. Its exact 0.5 purchases and level unlocks need fresh verification.

SPECIALIST EQUIPMENT

Banshee

Historically used for specialist weapons. Community 0.5 report: Glock 18C can appear here, with task and vendor progression affecting availability.

NOW REGULARLY ACCESSIBLE

Vulture

Confirmed change: no longer rotates between major Combat Outposts. Vulture is accessible through standard vendor access, including at base camp. Community 0.5 report: AUG appears in stock.

NEW TO THE ECONOMY

Progression is more than sales

Task rewards and presets

Check the requirements for the specific weapon you want. Some presets were changed to unlock via tasks, and early Supporter Preview observations can be outdated.

Smuggling and reputation

Delivering contraband through the new activity can earn both money and vendor reputation. Hunters can earn reputation through opposing objectives.

Crafting-first products

Tracking Tags and Stims moved from vendor shelves into crafting. Plan your Hideout instead of searching a trader for those items.

Old price / reputation math

The previous site assumed sale proceeds × 0.006 reputation. That was a community estimate from 0.4 and is not verified for 0.5, so it is no longer presented as current advice.

HOW WE VERIFIED THIS

Official versus community

Official source: 0.5 full changelog and economy and weapon changes. Recent player observations: 23 October new-guns-at-vendors report. Community stock may differ between ranks and patches.

Historical 0.4 “preferred buyer” lists and reputation conversion formulas are retained only as a warning here, not as confirmed recommendations for 0.5.
