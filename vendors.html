TRADING & REPUTATION

Sell with
purpose.

Use ordinary loot to raise the vendor whose unlocks you need. Current community consensus is to bring everyone to level 2, then prioritize Gunny and Handshake.

G

PRIORITY 1

Gunny

Weapons, suppressors and especially stronger 5.56 ammunition make Gunny the most useful early specialization for a Western-rifle path.

  • Push after all vendors reach level 2
  • Reported preference: alcohol, cigarettes and perfume
  • Strong route toward DDM4-style loadouts
H

PRIORITY 2

Handshake

Prioritize for armour and Western gear. Community advice commonly pairs Handshake level 3 with Gunny level 3.

  • Useful armour progression
  • Western equipment focus
  • Good second destination for sale reputation
LR

PRIORITY 3

Lab Rat

Useful medical access, including blood-bag progression. A practical follow-up once ammunition and armour are stable.

  • Medical supplies
  • Reported preference: phones
  • Raise earlier if healing access is limiting you
A

UTILITY

Artisan

Community recommendations call out Artisan level 3 for the key case—valuable quality of life for loot and locked-room runs.

  • Civilian equipment and containers
  • Level 3 key-case target
  • Reported preference: credit cards
T

PLAYSTYLE

Turncoat

More attractive for an AK/AK-308 path and larger-bag progression. Lower priority if you prefer Western rifles.

  • AK-oriented equipment
  • Delta bag route
  • Reported preference: jewellery
B

LATER / SPECIALIST

Banshee

Offers powerful later weapons including the M14 family. Recent community testing reports boosted prices for evidence items.

  • Later DMR and specialist equipment
  • Reported preference: evidence
  • Passport reports vary by patch/vendor
V

SECRET VENDOR

Vulture

Accepts high-value loot for reduced cash while building Vulture reputation. Use him when his progression matters more than maximum immediate payout.

  • High-value loot only
  • Reduced payout
  • Separate reputation objective
