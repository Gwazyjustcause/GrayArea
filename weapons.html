0.4 SPEARHEAD WEAPON GUIDE

Pick a rifle
you can feed.

A good gun is the one you can supply with armour-piercing ammunition, replace after a death, and use at the distances you actually fight. Start cheap; chase blueprints once your economy is stable.

AMMUNITION CHEAT SHEET

Load for the armour you face

5.45×39AK-74 / AK-12
PS→PP→BP→BS

Move toward BP/BS for armoured enemies. This calibre is attractive because enemies can replenish both rifles and ammunition.

5.56×45DDM4 / M4 / L403A1
M193→M855→M855A1→M995

Gunny progression matters because the DDM4 only becomes a true high-tier answer when it is paired with penetrating ammunition.

7.62×39AKM / AK-15 / SKS
SP→PS→BP→BZ

PS is adequate for early enemies; move to BP/BZ as protection improves. Avoid treating soft-point rounds as armour ammunition.

7.62×51AK-308 / M14 / M700
M80→M80A1→M61

Powerful and expensive. Carry it when the target or route justifies a premium loadout, not for routine starter-town farming.

The exact 0.4 ballistic values are not exposed in our source data. These ladders show the practical penetration progression commonly used by the community; patches can change individual rounds.

BUILDING THE GUN

Spend in this order

1Correct ammunition

Penetration for the enemy tier comes before every attachment.

2Usable optic

Red dot for buildings; 2.5–4× general use; variable optic for deliberate range.

3Suppressor

Reduces the attention generated by a fight. Verify the required muzzle device first.

4Control or ergonomics

Recoil for bursts; ergonomics for faster handling and longer scanning. Do not destroy handling for a tiny recoil gain.
