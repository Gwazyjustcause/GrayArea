PRACTICAL BUYING DECISIONS

Choose by purpose

EARLY / ECONOMICAL

Field-replaceable rifle

Keep using common AK or AR platforms when you can reliably source ammunition and spare magazines. A recoverable loadout beats losing an irreplaceable collection piece.

GENERAL PURPOSE

5.56 rifle family

DDM4, AUG, G36 and FAMAS provide different handling and modification choices. Compare the exact build, not just the platform name.

LONGER LINES

7.62 battle rifles

G3 and existing DMRs are worth evaluating for deliberate engagements. Heavier ammunition, recoil and equipment loss make them situational.

INTERIORS

SMG or shotgun

UMP and Benelli M4 diversify short-range options. Test the ammunition and effective range yourself before taking them into Tier 4 areas.

WEAPON PARTS

Customisation expanded

About 370 new parts were added, including magnifiers and additional components for older platforms. Compatibility and durability vary by build.

AVAILABILITY

Loot plus task unlocks

Many new weapons are found in-world; some presets are available through task completion and trader progress. “Loot only” reports from the preview may be outdated.

HOW TO BUILD FOR 0.5

Spend in the right order

1

Ammunition first

Use the correct calibre and penetration for the armour you expect. Ammunition quality still matters more than cosmetic rarity.

2

Practical optic

Choose sight picture and magnification for your route. The 0.5 update also provides more scope-sensitivity options.

3

Control and weight

Use recoil and ergonomics attachments only where they improve your actual fights. Extra weight has a cost on long material runs.

4

Repairability

World-looted weapons may arrive worn. Check durability, parts availability and cost before committing to an expensive new favourite.
